Jacob Lash is an asshole, and a Hero in Deadlock. The Lash is a brutal bruiser who eagerly initiates fights and chases down fleeing opponents. He leaps into combat with his Grappling hook, Flogging his foes with a life-stealing whip and stomping the ground with a Ground Strike that damages multiple targets. If the enemy team groups up, the Lash can pick them all up at once and Death Slam them into the ground.

A stevedore with ego the size of an airship, the Lash is more famous for his reputation as the champion of the underground fight club Bear Pit.
