Baby Globe is Wikipedia's 25th anniversary mascot. It was featured as a temporary birthday mascot of Wikipedia's 25th anniversary celebration. For a duration of time, Baby Globe would appear on select pages as an Easter Egg. The feature was available on a total of 27 Wikipedias across different languages. Baby Globe returned in May for the 25th Birthday Reading Challenge.

The character was originally conceived by the Wikimedian Jonathan Ferreira and introduced on Wikimedia blogs and the 25th birthday livestream.

Baby Globe's favourite colour is hyperlink blue, "as in the blue that links together articles across Wikipedia", but dislikes missing-link red. Baby Globe's original placeholder name/nickname was "the little mascot", given to it by its creator. How cute!
